I just finished listening to Rosamund Pike's narration of *The Shadow Rising*,
the fourth book in *The Wheel of Time* series. It was an epic experience yet
again that I highly recommend. That mesmerizing voice, pacing, world building
and the story really came together to keep me hooked.

Unfortunately, Rosamund Pike did not record the fifth book (yet? I can only
hope!) so I will read it instead, and I suspect I’ll hear her voice in my head
as I read. I listened to a sample from another audiobook, but it did not feel as
engaging to me.
