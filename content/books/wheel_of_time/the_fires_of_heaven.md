The fifth book in *The Wheel of Time* series was another epic experience, though
I dearly missed Perrin in this one. He is one of my favorite characters but this
book did not give him a single point-of-view chapter! I also missed Rosamund
Pike's narration, but had fun reading the story.

The pacing was good and there was quite a lot of world building again which kept
me hooked. The battles were action packed, and I quite enjoyed Moiraine's arc!
